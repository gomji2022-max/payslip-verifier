Rules & assumptions

This app's calculations are an estimate based on the EBA clause summary you provided (41.7, 41.8.1, 41.9.1(d), 44.2.1). They may not match your real payslip exactly. In particular, the following items have uncertain trigger conditions and are deliberately excluded from auto-calculation — enter them under "Other items" instead: SPEC-PENLTY, 50%/100% NIL 48, A/LVE PREM, and various leave/TOIL codes. Always treat your real payslip and People Services (1300 303 411) as the source of truth.
